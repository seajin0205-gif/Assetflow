Case 01 · Dashboard Chart

Show the year first. The rest of the chart can wait.

User Problem

Return, period, and every point at once. The first screen has no headline, and the phone has nowhere to look.

Interaction Decision

Open on one number. Change the period, and the line follows.

Expected Outcome

Year-to-date is the summary. The exact day appears only when you scrub to it.

01
Context — start with the year

02
Update — the line follows

A new period redraws the return and the shape together.

03
Explore — hover or touch a day

Scrub a point and that day’s value and return appear at once.

Case 02 · Trade Order

An order should answer. Check it, then say what happened.

User Problem

After the tap, it is unclear whether the order went through. A bad input does not say what to fix.

Interaction Decision

Check before submit. Failure and success each get their own toast, right away.

Expected Outcome

An error names the cause. A success shows the ticker, the size, and the amount.

01
Input — side and size

Pick buy or sell, then the quantity. An empty field keeps the button quiet.

02
Validate — three checks

Quantity, holdings, then limit price. A miss gets a short toast that names the cause.

03
Confirm — it landed

Success puts the ticker, the size, and the amount in one toast.

Case 03 · Mood Tracker

A mood should stay on the risk chart.

User Problem

There is no place to record how the market felt. Later, the risk chart has no context.

Interaction Decision

Five moods answer the moment you tap, and the chart marker stays in step with the selection.

Expected Outcome

One tap records the feeling. The chart can be read back to that moment.

The mood becomes a point on the risk chart, so the context stays.
